  one was found surfaced four more; generic rounds had missed them.

  ids are public, so the faker's id is always present. Stage 3.6.1 tests
  indistinguishability instead (public state identical across word/faker).

  owner question 0.8.3.
- A socket blip during drawing would cancel a round under a literal reading
  of §7; also in 0.8.3 (grace window).
